T Alerts SMS Terms & Conditions

Last updated: August 11, 2026

Program Description

T Alerts is a hobbyist-operated monitoring application that provides invited users with recurring automated, non-marketing SMS notifications about website availability and service status.

Users receive alerts only for monitored resources that they selected or administer. Messages may include outage detections, recovery notices, and service-status changes.

Eligibility and Consent

Enrollment is limited to invited users. To subscribe, an invited user must text JOIN from the mobile number that will receive alerts to the SMS number shown on the SMS program page. An invitation alone does not enroll a user, and no alert messages are sent before the user texts JOIN.

By texting JOIN, you confirm that you control or are authorized to use the subscribing mobile number and agree to receive recurring automated messages from Trey Gaines Alerts. Consent is voluntary and is not a condition of purchasing any goods or services.

Message Frequency and Charges

Message frequency varies according to the monitored resources selected by the user and the availability or status events generated by those resources.

Message and data rates may apply according to your wireless carrier and service plan.

Opt Out

You may unsubscribe at any time by replying STOP. After opting out, you will receive a confirmation message and no further alert messages unless you resubscribe by replying START.FT

Help

For assistance, reply HELP or INFO, or review the SMS program information.
