T Alerts Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 11, 2026

About This Service

T Alerts is a hobbyist-operated monitoring application that sends automated, non-marketing website availability and service-status notifications to invited users who have explicitly subscribed.

Users receive alerts only for monitored resources that they selected or administer.

Information Collected

The service may process a subscriber's mobile phone number, SMS opt-in or opt-out status, selected monitoring configuration, and message delivery information needed to operate and support the alert service.

How Information Is Used

Information is used only to provide, operate, maintain, secure, and support T Alerts. This includes associating a subscriber with selected monitored resources, sending requested alerts, honoring STOP requests, and responding to HELP requests.

Mobile Information and SMS Consent

Mobile phone numbers, SMS opt-in information, and messaging consent are not sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Messaging service providers and wireless carriers may process mobile information only as needed to transmit, deliver, secure, and support the requested messages. SMS consent is specific to T Alerts and is not transferred to another messaging program.

SMS Messaging Choices

Invited users subscribe by texting JOIN. Subscribers may reply STOP at any time to unsubscribe and may reply HELP or INFO for assistance. Message frequency varies based on configured monitoring events. Message and data rates may apply.

Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the alert service, maintain opt-in and opt-out records, resolve delivery or support issues, and meet applicable legal or compliance obligations.

Policy Updates

This policy may be updated to reflect changes to the application or its messaging practices. The current version and its effective date will remain available on this page.
