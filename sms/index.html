Trey Gaines Alerts SMS Program

Opt-in instructions and messaging disclosures for invited users.

About Trey Gaines Alerts

Trey Gaines Alerts is a hobbyist-operated monitoring application that sends automated, non-marketing website availability and service-status notifications to invited users who have explicitly subscribed.

Messages are triggered automatically when a configured website or service becomes unavailable, recovers, or reports a status change. Users receive alerts only for monitored resources that they selected or administer.

Enrollment is limited to invited users. The program does not use purchased telephone lists and does not send marketing or promotional messages.

How to Opt In

An invited user who wants to receive alerts must manually send the keyword JOIN from the mobile number that will receive messages to:

Program: Trey Gaines Alerts
SMS Number: +1 971-431-7643
Opt-In Keyword: JOIN

An invitation alone does not enroll a user. No alert messages are sent until the user texts JOIN. By texting JOIN, the user agrees to receive recurring automated website availability and service-status alerts from Trey Gaines Alerts.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Consent is voluntary and is not a condition of purchasing any goods or services. Review the Terms and Conditions and Privacy Policy before subscribing.

After JOIN is received, the application sends:

Trey Gaines Alerts: You are subscribed to recurring automated website availability and service-status alerts for monitored resources you select or administer. Message frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help or STOP to unsubscribe.

Types of Messages

Messages may include:

  • Website or service unavailability alerts
  • Website or service recovery notifications
  • Service-health and operational status changes

Alerts are associated only with resources that the subscriber selected or administers.

Message Frequency and Charges

Message frequency varies according to configured resources and the availability or status events generated by those resources.

Message and data rates may apply according to the subscriber's wireless carrier and service plan.

Opt Out

A subscriber may unsubscribe at any time by replying STOP.

Additional supported opt-out keywords include:

CANCEL END OPTOUT QUIT REVOKE STOP STOPALL UNSUBSCRIBE

After opting out, the subscriber receives:

Trey Gaines Alerts: You have been unsubscribed and will receive no further messages. Reply START to resubscribe.

A former subscriber may subsequently resubscribe by replying START or UNSTOP.

Help

A subscriber may reply HELP or INFO for assistance.

The application responds:

Trey Gaines Alerts: Automated website availability and service-status notifications for invited users. For help, visit https://trey-gaines.github.io/sms/. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to unsubscribe.

Privacy

Mobile phone numbers, SMS opt-in information, and messaging consent are not sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Messaging providers and wireless carriers may process mobile information only as needed to transmit, deliver, secure, and support requested messages.
