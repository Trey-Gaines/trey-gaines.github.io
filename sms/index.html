Trey Gaines Alerts — SMS Opt-In

Consent and messaging information for the Trey Gaines Alerts personal automated notification service.

About the Messaging Service

Trey Gaines Alerts is a private, non-marketing messaging service used to deliver automated informational alerts from personal applications and monitoring services.

Messages may include website availability and uptime alerts, application and system events, monitoring notifications, and news alerts based on topics configured by the recipient.

This service is intended solely for the campaign owner's personal use. It is not offered for public enrollment, and messages are not sent to customers, purchased telephone lists, third parties, or members of the general public.

How to Opt In

The sole authorized recipient provides explicit consent by manually sending the keyword JOIN from their personal mobile phone to:

SMS Number: +1 971-431-7643
Opt-In Keyword: JOIN

No automated campaign alert messages are sent to the recipient until the recipient has opted in.

After JOIN is received, the messaging service is configured to send the following confirmation:

Trey Gaines Alerts: You are subscribed to recurring automated personal website, news, application, monitoring, and system alerts. Message frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help or STOP to unsubscribe.

Message Frequency and Charges

Message frequency varies depending on configured website, news, application, monitoring, and system events.

Message and data rates may apply according to the recipient's wireless carrier and service plan.

Opt Out

The recipient may unsubscribe at any time by replying STOP.

Additional supported opt-out keywords include:

CANCEL END OPTOUT QUIT REVOKE STOP STOPALL UNSUBSCRIBE

After opting out, the recipient receives:

Trey Gaines Alerts: You have successfully unsubscribed and will receive no further messages. Reply START to resubscribe.

The recipient may subsequently re-subscribe using START or UNSTOP.

Help

The recipient may reply HELP or INFO for assistance.

The messaging service is configured to respond:

Trey Gaines Alerts: Help for the personal automated alert service. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to unsubscribe.
